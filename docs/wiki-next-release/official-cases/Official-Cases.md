# Official CYM Cases

The **Official CYM Cases** are the collection supplied by CYM co-developer [@birolt29](https://github.com/birolt29). The original manuals credit **@birolt29 and @alen29**; the Thingiverse package README/license files identify the uploader as `alen29`. Both original credits are preserved without assuming the relationship between the handles.

Manual languages: **EN — English; DE — German; TR — Turkish**. Each of the six case packages includes all three manuals, print models and original photos/design views.

The supplied case packages use **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**, independently of the CYM firmware license. Preserve attribution and share the supplied files unmodified. Commercial use or distributing modified designs requires permission from the rights holders. [License terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/).

## Assembly and compatibility boundaries

- **Official** identifies CYM’s preferred case collection; it is not a claim that every assembly or peripheral has passed CYM hardware acceptance. Select the exact board and revision; cases are not interchangeable across C5, original ESP32, and S3 boards.
- The two explicitly labelled **RF-HAT** packages are designed for the NM-CYD-C5 or Classic ESP32-2432S028R stacked with NM-RF-HAT. The original four battery/GPS board-only packages do not establish RF-HAT fit. Match the exact board, HAT revision, wiring and battery dimensions; mechanical design intent is not blanket CYM peripheral qualification.
- **Classic CYD GPS wiring requires reconciliation:** the original board-only 2432S028R manual describes CN1 (IO22/IO27). Jim’s known-good CYM harness uses receive-only GPIO1/P3. Do not repin a working harness merely to match the case manual; verify the exact firmware profile, connector pinout, voltage and continuity first. This caveat is separate from the unmodified manual, and is not a new approved pin map.
- **Hosyond Standard vs Slim:** speaker accommodation is a mechanical feature; it does not establish CYM audio support. Standard includes the supplied speaker; Slim omits it. S3 UART GPS, battery/SD and other peripheral qualification retain the board’s documented gates.
- Battery polarity, protection/charging and regulator output must be checked before connecting the board. Follow the exact board/package parts list; do not substitute a charging/boost arrangement solely because another case looks similar.

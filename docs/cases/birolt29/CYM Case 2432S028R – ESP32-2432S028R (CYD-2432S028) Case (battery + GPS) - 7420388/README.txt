CYM Case 2432S028R – ESP32-2432S028R (CYD-2432S028) Case (battery + GPS) by alen29 on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7420388

Summary:
This 3D-printable, two-part enclosure houses an ESP32-2432S028R board (the "CYD" with a 2.8" resistive touch screen, USB-C and micro-USB) for the Cheap Yellow Monster (CYM) firmware — https://github.com/JimGat/CYM (select the board as CYD-2432S028 in the Web Flasher). It turns the bare board into a pocket device with its own battery, charging and GPS.What fits insideThe ESP32-2432S028R main board, held by 4 × M3 × 7 screws from the front into the brass hex standoffs that come with the board's kitA 1100 mAh LiPo battery (3.7 V, 1.25 mm plug, up to 53 × 30.5 × 7.5 mm)A Youmile LX-LCBST charge + 5 V boost module (USB-C charging)An ATGM336H GPS module with its antennaAn SS12D00G slide switch (3 mm lever) and the screen stylusFeaturesTwo parts: a lower case and a front frame that wraps the screen and sits flush with itFlexible BOOT and RESET buttons on the back faceRoofed pocket for the GPS antenna and a routed channel for its cablePockets for the battery, charge module and GPS module; a pocket for the slide switch (O / I)A full-length channel for the stylus1.5 mm holes for the RGB LED, the two charge LEDs and the GPS LEDA hole in the front frame for the on-board light sensorEngraved port labels: MICRO, USB-C, P5, SD, CN1, P3, SPK, CHG, and O / IThe CHG (charge) port is charging-only and carries no data — see the manualPrintingTwo parts, printed flat, no supportsCase 88.9 × 52.9 × 15.8 mm; front frame 88.9 × 52.9 × 3.5 mm; combined thickness 19.3 mmAbout 20 g of PLAFull slicer settings are in the Print Settings belowAssemblyFull illustrated instructions are in the attached manual (EN / TR / DE, PDF): general information (board specifications, pinout, parts list), 3D printing, assembly and flashing the firmware with the CYM Web Flasher. It covers the wiring (GPS → CN1: GND · IO22 · IO27 · 3.3V, battery → charge module, module 5 V output → switch → board S3/S1), setting the module output to 5.0 V before assembly, and changing the on-board charge-current resistor to 2 kΩ (≈580 mA). Note: on this board the GPS module is powered from 3.3 V on CN1, not from the 4-pin P5 socket used on the NM-CYD-C5 — follow the manual.License: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 — you may print and use the case yourself and share the unmodified files with credit.

CYM Case WS-C5-28 – Waveshare ESP32-C5-Touch-LCD-2.8 Case (battery + GPS) by alen29 on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7417770

Summary:
This 3D-printable enclosure houses a Waveshare ESP32-C5-Touch-LCD-2.8 board (ESP32-C5, 2.8" IPS capacitive touch screen) for the Cheap Yellow Monster (CYM) firmware — https://github.com/JimGat/CYM (select the board as WS-C5-28 in the Web Flasher). It turns the bare board into a pocket device with its own battery and GPS.What fits insideThe Waveshare ESP32-C5-Touch-LCD-2.8 board, held by 4 × M2.5 × 16 cheese-head screws from the back into the board's own standoffsA 1100 mAh LiPo battery, 53 × 30 × 7.5 mm (measured, including protection board and cable exit), plugged into the board's own BAT socket — the board charges it over USB-C; no extra charge module neededAn ATGM336H GPS module (16 × 14 × 5.5 mm) with its antenna (19 × 6 × 5.5 mm), on the board's UART socket (GND · 3V3 · TXD · RXD)Power on/off with the board's own BAT_PWR buttonFeaturesSingle part; the board's glass sits inside a frame, flush with the edgeThree flexible buttons in the side wall for BOOT, RST and BAT (power)Pockets for the battery and the GPS module, an opening in the top wall for the GPS antenna, and a flat channel for the GPS cablemicroSD slot with a tweezer notch1.5 mm holes for the three GPS LEDs and the three microphonesOpenings for UART, USB-C and I2C; engraved labels UART, TYPE-C, I2C, GPS, CYM, BOOT, RST, BAT and SDPrintingOne part, printed flat on its closed back face, no supports53.0 × 75.5 × 20.2 mm, about 16 g of PLA, roughly 1 h 45 minAssemblyFull illustrated instructions are in the attached manual (EN / TR / DE, PDF, Revision 4): general information (board specifications, labeled pinout, parts list), 3D printing, a one-page wiring diagram, 13 assembly steps and flashing the firmware with the CYM Web Flasher.Revision 4 (2026-10): manuals moved to the common CYM case template; labeled pinout and wiring diagram added; screws corrected to M2.5 × 16.License: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 — you may print and use the case yourself and share the unmodified files with credit.

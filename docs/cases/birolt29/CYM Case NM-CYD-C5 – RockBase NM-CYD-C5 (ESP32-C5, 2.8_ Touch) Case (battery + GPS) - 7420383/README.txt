CYM Case NM-CYD-C5 – RockBase NM-CYD-C5 (ESP32-C5, 2.8" Touch) Case (battery + GPS) by alen29 on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7420383

Summary:
This 3D-printable, two-part enclosure houses a RockBase NM-CYD-C5 board (ESP32-C5 with a 2.8" resistive touch screen) for the Cheap Yellow Monster (CYM) firmware — https://github.com/JimGat/CYM (select the board as NM-CYD-C5 in the Web Flasher). It turns the bare board into a pocket device with its own battery, charging and GPS.What fits insideThe NM-CYD-C5 main board, held by 4 × M3 × 7 screws from the front into the brass hex standoffs that come with the board's kitA 1100 mAh LiPo battery (3.7 V, 1.25 mm plug, up to 53 × 30.5 × 7.5 mm)A Youmile LX-LCBST charge + 5 V boost module (USB-C charging)An ATGM336H GPS module with its antennaAn SS12D00G slide switch (3 mm lever) and the screen stylusFeaturesTwo parts: a lower case and a front frame that wraps the screen and sits flush with itFlexible BOOT and RESET buttons on the back faceRoofed pocket for the GPS antenna and a routed channel for its 90 mm cablePockets for the battery, charge module and GPS module; a pocket for the slide switch (O / I)A full-length channel for the stylus1.5 mm holes for the RGB LED, the two charge LEDs and the GPS LEDEngraved port labels: USB-C UART, USB-C, P5, SD, CN1, P1, SPK, CHG, and O / IThe CHG (charge) port is charging-only and carries no data — see the manualPrintingTwo parts, printed flat, no supportsCase 88.9 × 52.9 × 15.8 mm; front frame 88.9 × 52.9 × 3.5 mm; combined thickness 19.3 mmAbout 20 g of PLA; roughly 2 h 25 min for both parts togetherFull slicer settings are in the Print Settings belowAssemblyFull illustrated instructions are in the attached manual (EN / TR / DE, PDF): general information (board specifications, pinout, parts list), 3D printing, assembly and flashing the firmware with the CYM Web Flasher. It covers the wiring (GPS → P5, battery → charge module, module 5 V output → switch → board S3/S1), setting the module output to 5.0 V before assembly, and changing the on-board charge-current resistor to 2 kΩ (≈580 mA) to suit the 1100 mAh battery.License: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 — you may print and use the case yourself and share the unmodified files with credit.

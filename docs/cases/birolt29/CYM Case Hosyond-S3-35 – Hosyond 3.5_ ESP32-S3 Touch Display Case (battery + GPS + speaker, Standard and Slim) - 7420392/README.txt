CYM Case Hosyond-S3-35 – Hosyond 3.5" ESP32-S3 Touch Display Case (battery + GPS + speaker, Standard and Slim) by alen29 on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7420392

Summary:
This 3D-printable, two-part enclosure houses a Hosyond 3.5" ESP32-S3 capacitive touch display board (ES3C35P, 320×480) for the Cheap Yellow Monster (CYM) firmware — https://github.com/JimGat/CYM (select the board as Hosyond ES3C35P 3.5 in the Web Flasher). It turns the bare board into a pocket device with its own battery, GPS and speaker.Two models, one front coverStandard (case 15.0 mm): with the speaker from the board set, facing a grille in the back.Slim (case 12.9 mm): no speaker, thinner.What fits insideThe Hosyond 3.5" ESP32-S3 board (54.5 × 101.5 mm), held by 4 × M3 screws (6–7 mm) into M3 brass hex standoff nuts (5 mm) in the case posts — the same screws and nuts as in the CYM NM-CYD-C5 and 2432S028R cases; they are not included in the Hosyond setA 1100 mAh LiPo battery, 53 × 30 × 7.5 mm, plugged into the board's own BAT socket — the board charges it over USB-C; no extra charge module neededAn ATGM336H GPS module with its antenna, on the board's UART socketThe 40.5 × 28.5 × 10 mm speaker from the board set (Standard only), on the SPK socketAn SS12D00G slide switch in the battery leadFeaturesFlexible BOOT and RESET buttons on the back faceRoofed pocket for the GPS antenna; pockets for the battery, GPS module, speaker and switchHex nut pockets with small crush ribs that hold the nutHoles for the RGB LED and the GPS LED; speaker grille (Standard)Side openings for UART, I2C, EXP.INT, microSD and USB-C; engraved labelsPrintingTwo parts per model, printed flat, no supportsStandard: 57.4 × 105.4 × 15.0 mm + front cover 3.9 mm (18.9 mm total); Slim: 12.9 + 3.9 = 16.8 mmAbout 21–24 g of PLA; roughly 3 h (Standard) / 2 h 30 min (Slim) for both parts togetherFull slicer settings are in the Print Settings below and in the manualAssemblyFull illustrated instructions are in the attached manual (EN / TR / DE, PDF).License: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 — you may print and use the case yourself and share the unmodified files with credit.
